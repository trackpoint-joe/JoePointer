Executive Profile

Adoption is personal before it is organizational. Someone adopts AI when they rethink how their own work could be done, not when they are handed a license. I measure whether that happened by active usage, not headcount with access. At TIAA I reported the 1,500+ associates I reached directly while 16,000+ had access, because the second number flatters and the first one is true. I was there fifteen years, in a business where Risk, Compliance, and Legal could stop anything I built. Earlier, ten years at Pearson and Vangent serving Fortune 500 enterprises and federal agencies. My M.S. in Instructional Technology is in how people learn and decide, and how to design the experience that gets them there.
